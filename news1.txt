New Delhi: The BJP on Saturday accused the INDIA bloc of making a "mockery" of Parliament and democracy after the opposition alliance held a symbolic "public impeachment" of CEC Gyanesh Kumar, and said that the Congress will not succeed no matter how much it tries to "launch" its leader Rahul Gandhi.

Addressing a press conference at the BJP headquarters here, he accused the Congress of spreading lies about alleged irregularities in the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls and the functioning of the Election Commission.
